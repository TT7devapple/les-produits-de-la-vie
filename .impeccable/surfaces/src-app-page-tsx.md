Constraints: no invented claims, no prices, CC0 photos are temporary.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the paper bag you carry out of the shop: raw kraft printed in a single flexo ink, and every product wears its scale label. It refuses the cream-serif-terracotta "épicerie fine" template and its hero/cards/CTA scaffold.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft field (#C7A06F with fibre texture) owns most surfaces; one ink, deep flexo blue (#1B2E80), prints all type, rules, stamps and duotone photos; knocked-out blue fields let kraft show through. White thermal labels (#F4F4F0, black print, mono data) carry live facts and products. Condensed grotesk caps, square corners, rubber-stamp marks for state.

STORY: A neighbour learns in one glance that this is a farm's own shop, sees today's status and the next Thursday arrival, finds the address under Métro Bérault, and walks in.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Kraft, bag-mouth serrated top. Headline in massive condensed blue caps across 8 columns; right column a stapled scale label printing live status (open until / next arrival / address). Primary action "Nous trouver" as solid ink block under the headline; secondary link to products. Mobile: headline, label full width, actions.

FORM: Kraft bag & bakery scale label, candidate 4 of 7, seed 84be0f42. Signature interaction: the label prints line by line once on load; stamps land once when scrolled into view.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
